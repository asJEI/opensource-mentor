OpenSource Mentor uses a dual-licensing model for material the project maintainer

1. **Open-source option — GNU AGPL version 3 only (`AGPL-3.0-only`).**
   The full, unmodified license is in [LICENSE](./LICENSE). You may use the
   software, including commercially, when you comply with that license. Source

   modify and how you provide it over a network; consult the actual license.
2. **Alternative commercial option — separately negotiated written agreement.**
   If your intended distribution, proprietary integration or hosted offering
   cannot comply with AGPL, contact the maintainer before proceeding. A signed
   agreement can grant alternative rights within its stated scope. This document
   does not itself grant a proprietary license, promise availability, set a price
   or waive any AGPL obligation.

AGPL is **not** a non-commercial license. Paying is not required merely because
an organization uses the software commercially while complying with AGPL.
Equally, not every private modification must be published to everyone. Applicable
source obligations must be evaluated under the license, including section 13 for
modified versions offered to remote users.

For licensing inquiries, use a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/asJEI/opensource-mentor/issues/new)
or the maintainer's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/asJEI) to request a contact
channel. Do not post private commercial details or credentials publicly. The
maintainer will confirm the contracting party and terms separately.

Third-party dependencies and third-party material retain their respective
licenses. A commercial agreement cannot waive third-party obligations unless
the necessary rights have separately been obtained. Public package metadata
uses `AGPL-3.0-only` to describe the public distribution; the commercial option
requires its own executed agreement.

## Contributions

Contributors retain their rights. Unless a separate agreement applies, accepted
contributions to the public project are under AGPL-3.0-only. Submitting a pull
request is not an automatic copyright assignment or permission for proprietary
relicensing. Before including another contributor's work in a commercial edition,
the maintainer must obtain any necessary additional written permissions or an
appropriate contributor agreement. No contributor agreement is supplied here.
